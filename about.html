Started in a garage, still run like one

The short version: we got tired of watching good clothes get skipped. The longer version is below.

Why we started

In 2021 we were helping a relative clear out a house and found three garbage bags of clothing headed straight for the dump — wool coats, barely-worn shoes, a leather jacket that just needed a new zipper. Most of it was fine. It just needed someone to look at it properly.

That became the whole idea: look at things properly before deciding they're done. We started selling out of that same garage to friends, then friends of friends, and it grew from there.

How we source and check every piece

Nothing here is bought in bulk sight-unseen.

01

Sourcing

Estate sale walk-throughs, donation partnerships, and direct closet buyouts. We look at the item, not just the bag it came in.

02

Cleaning & repair

Every piece is washed. Loose buttons get resewn, small tears get patched, and anything beyond fixing doesn't make the cut.

03

Honest tagging

We note the real condition — "small mark on cuff" means there's a small mark on the cuff. Pricing reflects brand, fabric, and condition, not hype.

What doesn't make it to the shop

Stained beyond a good wash, stretched-out elastic, broken zippers we can't source parts for, or anything that just doesn't have wear left in it — that gets set aside for textile recycling instead of landfill. We'd rather sell fewer pieces than sell you something you'll toss in a month.

We also skip fast-fashion pieces already falling apart at the seams. If it wasn't built to last the first time, a wash won't fix that.

Want to sell us your closet clear-out?

We take walk-ins by appointment and pay fair prices for pieces in good condition.

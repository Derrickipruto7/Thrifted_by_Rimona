How a piece gets to you

No mystery boxes. Here's the actual process, every time.

01

We source it

Estate sales, donation partners, and closet buyouts — we look for pieces with life left in them.

02

We check it

Every item is washed, inspected for damage, and mended if it needs a stitch or a button.

03

We're not a warehouse. We're three people and a very full garage.

Thriftly started because we hated watching good clothes get skipped over for something fast and forgettable. Every listing on this site has actually been touched, checked, and chosen by one of us — not scanned in bulk from a bale.
